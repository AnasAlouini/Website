Competitions

Before talking about the competitions we need to know the two main types of competitions

Road Races

Road races take place on paved surfaces like streets and city roads. They are the best starting point for beginners because the terrain is flat and predictable, making it easier to control your pace and avoid injury. The most common beginner road races are the 5K and 10K, which require no special gear beyond a comfortable pair of running shoes.

Trail Races

Trail races take place in nature — on dirt paths, forests, and mountains. They are more physically demanding due to uneven terrain, elevation changes, and obstacles like rocks and roots. Beginners should attempt trail races only after building a solid running base of at least 3 to 6 months. A pair of trail shoes with good grip is essential.

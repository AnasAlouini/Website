Types of Races

🏙️ Road Races

Road races take place on paved surfaces like streets and city roads. They are the best starting point for beginners because the terrain is flat and predictable. The most common beginner road races are the 5K and 10K, which require no special gear beyond a comfortable pair of running shoes.

🌲 Trail Races

Trail races take place in nature — on dirt paths, forests, and mountains. They are more physically demanding due to uneven terrain and elevation changes. Beginners should attempt trail races only after building a solid running base of at least 3 to 6 months. Trail shoes with good grip are essential.
